Google Analytics: Understand What Your Website Visitors Are Really Doing

31 July 2026 3 min read Sem van Wijk

A website is much more than an online business card. To get the most out of it, you need to understand how visitors interact with your pages. Which pages attract the most attention? Where do your visitors come from? Why do some people leave without contacting you? Google Analytics provides the answers.

What is Google Analytics?

Google Analytics is a free analytics platform developed by Google that helps you understand how people use your website. It collects visitor data and presents it in easy to understand reports, allowing you to make informed decisions based on real data instead of assumptions.

Whether you run a business website, portfolio, blog or online store, Google Analytics helps you identify what works well and where improvements can be made.

What can you measure?

Google Analytics offers detailed insights, including:

  • Total visitors over any period
  • Traffic sources such as Google Search, social media, email campaigns and advertisements
  • The most visited pages
  • Average time visitors spend on each page
  • Clicks on buttons and links
  • Device types including desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Visitor locations
  • Exit pages and user journeys

Together, these insights create a complete picture of how people interact with your website.

How does it help?

The collected data allows you to improve both your website and your marketing efforts.

You can identify pages that attract many visitors but generate few enquiries, discover which campaigns deliver the best return, and understand where users leave your website before completing an action.

For ecommerce websites, Google Analytics can reveal where customers abandon the checkout process. Service based businesses can measure contact form submissions, phone call clicks and quotation requests.

This enables you to make smarter business decisions backed by reliable data.

Different ways to set it up

There are several ways to implement Google Analytics.

The simplest approach is adding the Google Analytics tracking code directly to your website.

Many businesses prefer using Google Tag Manager because it allows you to manage Analytics together with other tracking tools from one central platform.

If your website runs on WordPress or another content management system, you can often install Analytics through a plugin within just a few minutes.

For more advanced tracking, custom events and conversions can be configured. This makes it possible to measure actions such as downloading brochures, watching videos, submitting forms or completing purchases.

What insights can you gain?

The real value of Google Analytics comes from turning data into actionable insights.

For example, you may discover that most visitors browse your website on mobile devices. This could encourage you to further optimize the mobile experience.

You might also find that one particular page attracts significant traffic from search engines. Expanding that content could increase your organic visibility even further.

Analytics also helps identify which marketing channels generate the highest quality leads, allowing you to invest your budget more effectively.

Why is it important?

A visually appealing website is only the first step. Without data, it is difficult to know whether your website is actually delivering results.

Google Analytics helps you understand your audience, improve user experience, optimize marketing campaigns and increase conversions. By regularly reviewing your data and making informed improvements, your website becomes a stronger tool for growing your business.
